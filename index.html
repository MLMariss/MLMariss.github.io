About the tools

QTPD

QTPD (Quality Time Per Dollar) answers one question: which Steam games give you the most well-reviewed hours for your money? It combines each game's review score with how long it takes to play, divides by the current price, and ranks thousands of Steam games by the result. You can filter by price, discount, review score, length and tags, so it works for a big Steam sale as well as for finding a cheap game to sink a weekend into.

Dawnwalker skill tree planner

The skill tree planner for The Blood of Dawnwalker lays out all 54 perks, 9 ultimates and 27 abilities across the Witchcraft, Swordmastery and Vampirism trees. Spend points with the game's real rules (prerequisites, Corruption gates, manuals), pick an ultimate, and share the finished build as a link.
